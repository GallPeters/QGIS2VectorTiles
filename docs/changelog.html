v4.2

Coming soon
  • Balloon callouts of point labels, as the label's own background: drawn by QGIS with every layer of its fill symbol (outlines, and geometry generators such as an icon in its corner) and stretched around the text, so it fits at every zoom and is hidden with its label as in QGIS. Label signs keep their background.
  • The other callouts of labels placed at their point (around it or at an offset), not only at a data-defined position; a balloon's fill symbol drawn layer by layer there too.
  • Fixed: multi-line labels were always centred: their QGIS alignment (left, right, following the placement) is kept.
  • Fixed: spaces at the start or end of a label's lines (an indent, padding) were dropped by the web map, also after direction marks (as in mixed Hebrew lines).
  • Faster web map: labels appear at once, as in QGIS, without MapLibre's fade, which was most of the wait after each pan or zoom (about 9.6 s down to 1.6 s over 16 moves on a 2,800-sign layer); the local server also answers on IPv6, so a new connection to localhost no longer stalls.
  • Labels around a point placed as QGIS places them: their side or corner on the point, then the label distance (no extra gap), so a balloon's wedge and what its generators draw land where QGIS draws them.
  • Line spacing of multi-line labels exactly as QGIS lays them out, and a balloon around them as tall as QGIS's label box (room above the first line and below the last, whatever the line height).
  • Fixed: a stretched balloon showed shading from its outline and icon while zooming.
  • Fixed: a balloon sign placed "cartographic" moved to other positions on the web map, its pointer left floating; with one position it keeps it, where QGIS puts it (the distance from the symbol's bounds, the text's descent), its pointer on the point; with several (around point, or a cartographic order) it moves through them as in QGIS, drawn without its pointer (which can't turn with it).
  • Fixed: zoom bands beyond the highest tile zoom were drawn twice.

v4.1

Coming soon
  • Up to 2× faster on large projects.
  • Point clusters, point displacement, heatmaps, merged features and inverted polygons.
  • Label priority as in QGIS in both viewers, data-defined too.
  • Lighter, faster web maps: half-size styles, fewer layers (alike layers and zoom bands drawn as one), no needless sorting.
  • Outlines and point or line labels share their features' data in the tiles.
  • @map_scale rules make one layer in the tiles and the style: smaller tiles, faster drawing.
  • Arrows drawn as in QGIS: curved or straight, on every segment or not, half arrows, tapered.
  • Callouts (simple, Manhattan, curved, balloon) to labels at a data-defined position (X, Y), aligned there as in QGIS, data-defined alignment too.
  • Features drawn in the layer's rendering order.
  • Data-defined colours work out in C++, any number of them.
  • Rules on fields a layer lacks (a style shared by layers) export in C++ too.
  • Trimmed lines and fill offsets, data-defined too; outlines drawn inside polygons.
  • Markers turned with the line on vertices and segment centres; first and last markers offset along the line.
  • Scale ranges switch at the exact zoom, measured at the data's latitude.
  • Map-unit size limits; label backgrounds: rectangles, ellipses and SVGs.
  • Hatched fills keep their hatching.
  • Data-defined marker colours and shapes and font family.
  • Data-defined show label and symbol layer enabled.
  • Line labels once per line; merge connected lines.
  • Outside-polygon labels, title case and labels that wrap on a character.
  • Search shows one result per place.
  • The map opens fitted to the export extent.
  • The web map's server sends the style, scripts and search index compressed.
  • An export that crashes runs again by itself.
  • Fixed: road casings of categorized layers could disappear.
  • Fixed: some data-defined expressions crashed the export.
  • Fixed: letters missing from labels sharing a font.
  • Fixed: @map_scale in data-defined show label or enabled.
  • Fixed: scale ranges beyond zoom 15 wrote their features twice.

v4.0

14 Oct 2026 Latest
  • Publish anywhere: a static web map with one PMTiles file.
  • No tiles layer, .qlr or .mbtiles: the web map is the result.
  • One web map, two viewers: switch in the page.
  • Same zoom numbers in both viewers.
  • Reopens in the viewer you used last.
  • Tidier layout: settings left, zoom and drawing right.
  • About 40% faster on large projects.
  • Low zoom levels keep all their features.
  • OpenLayers zoom levels follow MapLibre's numbering.
  • No more blank map while zooming between levels.
  • Embed the web map anywhere with ?embed=1.
